# Data lifecycle

## Request a workspace export

A workspace owner can request a workspace export from Settings → Data → Export. Support sends the owner a download link by email when the export is ready. The download link expires after seven days.

## Audit history retention

Enterprise workspaces retain audit history for 365 days. Team workspaces retain audit history for 90 days. Owners can export audit events as CSV from Settings → Audit log.

## Delete a workspace

Workspace owners can request deletion from Settings → Data. The workspace is locked on confirmation and permanently deleted after 30 days. An owner can cancel the deletion during that period by contacting support.

## Data residency

EU workspaces store customer content and backups in Frankfurt. Data residency is selected when a workspace is created and cannot be changed in Settings.
