# Incident response

## Declare a P1

Declare P1 when all customer workspaces are unavailable or an incident causes confirmed customer data loss. The on-call engineer creates an incident record and pages the incident commander immediately.

## Post incident updates

The incident commander posts a status update every 30 minutes during an active P1. The status page update includes customer impact, the current response, and the next update time.

## Write a postmortem

The incident commander publishes the P1 postmortem within five business days of resolution. Include a timeline, contributing factors, and follow-up actions with owners.
