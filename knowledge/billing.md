# Billing and plans

## Download invoices

Only workspace owners and billing admins can download invoices. Open Settings → Billing → Invoices and select Download PDF next to the statement. Invoices are issued on the first day of the next billing period.

## Upgrade a plan

A plan upgrade takes effect immediately. The billing system prorates the difference for the remaining days in the current billing period. A scheduled downgrade takes effect at the next renewal.

## Update billing details

Workspace owners and billing admins can change the billing contact and address under Settings → Billing → Billing details. Changes appear on future invoices. Existing invoices remain unchanged.

## Failed card payment

If a card payment fails, update the payment method under Settings → Billing. The billing system retries the charge after 48 hours. Paid features remain available during the retry window.
