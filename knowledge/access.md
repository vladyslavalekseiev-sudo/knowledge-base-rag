# Workspace access

## SAML sign-in

SAML single sign-on is included in the Enterprise plan. A workspace owner configures the identity provider under Settings → Authentication. Keep password sign-in enabled until a second owner has tested the SAML connection.

## SCIM provisioning

SCIM provisioning is available on Enterprise workspaces. A workspace owner can create a SCIM token under Settings → Provisioning. A SCIM deactivation suspends workspace access immediately and preserves the user's records.

## Lost authenticator

When a workspace member loses their authenticator, a workspace owner can reset the member's two-factor authentication from the member profile. If all owners lose access, contact support to begin manual identity verification.

## Guest invitations

Guest invitations expire after 72 hours. The sender can issue a new invitation from Settings → Members. Guests can open shared reports and cannot change workspace settings.
